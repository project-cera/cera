## Implications for the SIL Component

1. **SIL halves the error rate**: cera-full at 28.0% vs cera-no-sil at 57.3% and heuristic at 55.4%.

2. **SIL eliminates severe confabulation**: cera-full has zero wrong-generation CPU claims, zero wrong-resolution display claims, and zero AMOLED hallucinations. These severe errors are common in both heuristic and cera-no-sil.

3. **SIL prevents existential denial**: Heuristic reviews uniquely deny real features (microSD reader, headphone jack, fingerprint scanner, USB-A ports). Neither CERA condition exhibits this.

4. **Without SIL, CERA is comparable to heuristic**: cera-no-sil (57.3%) and heuristic (55.4%) are close, confirming SIL is the key differentiator.

5. **Residual cera-full errors are naming/spec precision**: The remaining errors are 'Windows Studio Effects' vs 'Samsung Studio Mode' (correct concept, wrong name), Wi-Fi 6 vs 6E, and 720p vs 1080p webcam. These represent MAV coverage gaps rather than fundamental hallucination.

## Study Limitations

- **Single domain**: Laptop reviews only
- **Single LLM flagger**: Claude Opus 4.6 may have systematic biases
- **Single generation LLM**: All reviews generated by Qwen3.5-35B-A3B
- **Product lineup confusion**: Samsung Galaxy Book3 lineup creates natural confusability
- **Naming ambiguity**: 'Windows Studio Effects' vs 'Samsung Studio Mode' is a naming precision issue, not a fundamental hallucination
